# Sample data

Every file below is synthetic: invented clinical notes for ten radiology protocols, and the
tables the jobs derive from them, generated by [`generate_sample.py`](generate_sample.py) from
sentence templates (age and sex, a presenting complaint, a history, sometimes a sentence that
has nothing to do with the protocol). No real patient, note or record is used. Licence: CC0-1.0.
The folder is about 60 KB, far under the 5 MB per file and 20 MB per folder limits.

| File | What it is | Where it came from | Licence | Synthetic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `notes_raw.parquet` | 200 labelled notes, 20 per protocol: note_id, text, label | Generated by `generate_sample.py` | CC0-1.0 | yes |
| `notes_test_raw.parquet` | 100 held-out notes, 10 per protocol: note_id, text, ground_truth | Generated by `generate_sample.py` | CC0-1.0 | yes |
| `label_dataset.parquet` | The 200 distinct notes whose protocol occurs often enough to train on: note_id, text, label | Built from `notes_raw` by the `prepare_notes` job | CC0-1.0 | yes |
| `predictions.parquet` | The protocol suggested per test note, with confidence: note_id, predicted_label, confidence | Built by the `label_protocols` job from the recorded answers in `tests/recorded_answers.json`, which the generator writes (invented, see below) | CC0-1.0 | yes |
| `evaluation_summary.parquet` | Accuracy, correct and total over the 100 test notes (92 of 100) | Built by the `evaluate_predictions` job | CC0-1.0 | yes |
| `label_metrics.parquet` | Precision, recall and F1 per protocol, 10 rows | Built by the `evaluate_predictions` job | CC0-1.0 | yes |
| `confusion_matrix.parquet` | Actual against predicted protocol with note counts, 17 rows | Built by the `evaluate_predictions` job | CC0-1.0 | yes |
| `label_distribution.parquet` | Notes per protocol in the training set, 10 rows of 20 | Built by the `label_summary` job | CC0-1.0 | yes |

Ten radiology protocols are covered: CTA Aortic Dissection, CT Urography, Renal Stone Protocol,
C-Spine(Bone), L-Spine, Routine Sinus, Pulmonary Embolus, CTA Brain, CT Abdomen and Brain
Perfusion. No text occurs twice, in the training notes or between them and the test notes. Each
protocol has its own complaints and histories, and some notes carry only a vague complaint that
two protocols share ("acute chest pain", "flank pain", "back pain after a fall"), so they are
ambiguous between similar protocols: 4 of every 20 training notes and 2 of every 10 test notes.

The predictions are invented to exercise the metrics, so that accuracy, the per-protocol
metrics and the confusion matrix have something to show. They equal the ground truth except for
8 of the 100 test notes, chosen with a fixed seed among the ambiguous ones, which get the
protocol they are plausibly mixed up with (for example a pulmonary embolus read as an aortic
dissection, an abdominal pain read as a renal stone, a cervical read as a lumbar spine), with a
confidence lower than the correct ones. They are not the output of a model and say nothing about
any labelling method. `tests/recorded_answers.json` replays them, so `label_protocols` runs
without a model.

To regenerate (the same seed always gives the same files):

```
uv run python data/generate_sample.py
```
